## Document purpose

This manual explains how a rider registers, signs in, manages availability, views and updates deliveries, shares a live GPS location, reads notifications, creates an additional delivery request, updates profile details, and signs out of the SC Courier Rider App.

The names of buttons and screens in this guide match the current application. Screens may look slightly different after a future app update or on a different Android version.

# 1. About the app

The SC Courier Rider App helps a rider perform the main tasks required during a delivery shift. A rider can:

- Set their work availability.
- Receive and view assigned deliveries.
- Review pickup, drop-off, recipient, parcel, distance, and price details.
- Update each delivery through its progress stages.
- Share live GPS information while working.
- View pickup and drop-off points on a map.
- Receive assignment and delivery-change notifications.
- Create an additional delivery request.
- Maintain personal, contact, and vehicle information.

The app uses a dark interface. Orange is the main action colour, green normally indicates an active or completed state, blue indicates work waiting to be handled, and grey indicates an inactive state.

# 2. Before you begin

Make sure you have:

- An Android phone with the SC Courier Rider App installed.
- A working internet connection.
- Access to your email account.
- Your National Identity Card (NIC) number.
- Your current home address and emergency contact number.
- Your vehicle type, vehicle registration number, and driving licence number.
- GPS/location services enabled when using live tracking.

For the best results, allow notifications and location access when Android asks. The exact Android permission wording depends on the phone model and Android version.

> **Important:** Never share your password or email confirmation link with another person.

# 4. Signing in and account access

## 4.1 Sign in

1. Enter the email address used during registration.
2. Enter your password.
3. Tap **Login**.

After successful authentication, the Dashboard opens. An existing signed-in session may take you directly to the Dashboard when the app starts.

## 4.2 Reset a forgotten password

1. Enter your email address on the Login screen.
2. Tap **Forgot password?**
3. Open the reset message sent to that email address.
4. Follow the instructions in the message.

If no reset message arrives, check the Spam or Junk folder and confirm that the typed email address is correct.

## 4.3 Resend account confirmation

When the app identifies an unconfirmed account, it displays **Resend confirmation email**. Confirm that the correct email is entered, tap the option, and check your inbox.

# 6. Rider availability

Availability tells the dispatch/admin team whether you can receive new work.

## 6.1 Change availability

1. On the Dashboard, tap the rider card containing your name and availability badge.
2. Select the appropriate state.
3. Wait for the confirmation message before continuing.

> **INSERT SCREENSHOT 06 — Set your availability panel**  
> Suggested file name: `06-availability.png`

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Available | You are ready to receive new deliveries. |
| On delivery | You are currently working on a delivery. |
| Offline | You are not working and should not receive new assignments. |

Use **Available** only when you are ready to accept work. Set **Offline** at the end of the shift. Availability and GPS tracking are separate controls; changing availability does not automatically start or stop GPS tracking.

# 7. Assigned deliveries

## 7.1 View the delivery list

Assigned work appears under **Active Deliveries**. Each row can show:

- Delivery or tracking reference.
- Current status.
- Pickup address.
- Drop-off address.
- Recipient and price, when available.

Tap the refresh icon beside Active Deliveries if a new assignment has not appeared. You can also pull down to refresh the full Dashboard.

## 7.2 Open delivery details

Tap a delivery row to open **Delivery Details**.

> **INSERT SCREENSHOT 07 — Delivery Details information**  
> Suggested file name: `07-delivery-details.png`

The detail page is divided into:

- **Route:** pickup and drop-off addresses.
- **Recipient:** recipient name and telephone number.
- **Parcel:** package description, distance, and price.
- **Timeline:** created and last-updated times.
- **Actions:** GPS Tracking and delivery progress controls.

Use the refresh icon at the top of Delivery Details, or pull down on the page, to retrieve the latest information.

Before travelling, check the pickup and drop-off addresses, recipient details, and parcel information. If important information is missing or incorrect, contact dispatch before changing the delivery status.

# 8. Updating delivery progress

The delivery progress area contains four stages:

1. **Accepted** — the job has been accepted.
2. **Picked up** — the parcel has been collected.
3. **In transit** — the parcel is on the way to the recipient.
4. **Completed** — the parcel has been delivered to the recipient.

> **INSERT SCREENSHOT 08 — Delivery progress and Actions**  
> Suggested file name: `08-delivery-progress.png`

## 8.1 Recommended status sequence

1. Review the delivery information and mark **Accepted**.
2. After physically collecting the parcel, mark **Picked up**.
3. When leaving the pickup point for the recipient, mark **In transit**.
4. After successful handover to the correct recipient, mark **Completed**.

The current stage is labelled **Now** and the next stage can be labelled **Mark**. Wait for the “Delivery marked …” confirmation after selecting a stage.

> **Important:** Update statuses only when the real-world event has happened. Do not mark a parcel Completed before handover. Although other stages may be selectable, riders should follow the sequence above unless dispatch gives a different instruction.

# 9. GPS tracking

GPS Tracking shares the rider’s live position with the delivery system and displays route-related markers.

## 9.1 Start tracking

1. Tap the GPS status card on the Dashboard, or tap **Open GPS Tracking** in Delivery Details.
2. Tap **Start Tracking**.
3. If Android requests location access, select the permission required for work use.
4. Ensure the phone’s Device GPS/Location service is switched on.
5. Wait until the status changes to **GPS tracking active** and coordinates appear.

> **INSERT SCREENSHOT 09 — GPS Tracking screen**  
> Suggested file name: `09-gps-tracking.png`

The GPS screen can show:

- Your position on the map, labelled **You**.
- Pickup and drop-off markers derived from assigned deliveries.
- Latitude and longitude.
- Current speed in kilometres per hour.
- GPS accuracy in metres.
- A summary of active parcel locations.

Position updates are sent when tracking is active and the device moves. Keep mobile data and Location enabled. For stronger GPS reception, move outdoors or near a window.

## 9.2 Stop tracking

1. Open GPS Tracking.
2. Tap **Stop Tracking**.
3. Confirm that the page and Dashboard show that GPS tracking is off.

Stop tracking after the shift unless SC Courier procedures require it to remain active.

## 9.3 Fix permission or GPS problems

Open **Settings** from the Dashboard and use:

- **App Location Permission** to manage the Android permission for this app.
- **Device GPS** to open the phone’s Location settings.

If the message says the permission was permanently denied, open App Location Permission and allow location manually. If the app cannot obtain a position, move to a place with a clearer view of the sky and try again.

# 10. Notifications

The app stores an in-app copy of assignment and delivery-change notifications.

## 10.1 Open and use notifications

1. Tap the bell icon on the Dashboard.
2. Read the newest notification at the top of the list.
3. Tap a notification containing a delivery reference to open that delivery.
4. Use **Mark all read** to clear all unread indicators.
5. Pull down to refresh the inbox.

> **INSERT SCREENSHOT 10 — Notifications inbox**  
> Suggested file name: `10-notifications.png`

Unread notifications appear more prominently and include a coloured dot. The Dashboard bell can display an unread-count badge.

Notifications can include new assignments, reassignment of a trip, a delivery added to a trip, and delivery changes. Always open the affected delivery and verify its current details before travelling.

# 11. Adding a delivery request

The Dashboard’s **Add Delivery** panel opens the **Extend Delivery** screen.

## 11.1 Enter the request

1. On the Dashboard, tap **Add** in the Add Delivery panel.
2. Enter the **Sender Name**.
3. Enter the **Receiver Name**.
4. Enter the **Delivery Location**.
5. Enter the distance in kilometres.
6. Review the automatically calculated total price.
7. Tap **Submit Delivery**.

The current app calculates the displayed price at **LKR 50.00 per kilometre**.

- Tap **Submit** to create the request.

# 14. Recommended daily workflow

## Start of shift

1. Sign in and check that the displayed rider name is yours.
2. Review profile and vehicle details if anything has changed.
3. Enable mobile data and Device GPS.
4. Open GPS Tracking and tap Start Tracking.
5. Set availability to Available.
6. Review notifications and Active Deliveries.

## For each delivery

1. Open Delivery Details.
2. Verify route, recipient, and parcel information.
3. Mark Accepted.
4. Travel to the pickup point.
5. Collect and verify the parcel, then mark Picked up.
6. Mark In transit when leaving for the destination.
7. Hand the parcel to the correct recipient.
8. Mark Completed only after successful handover.
9. Refresh the Dashboard and continue with the next assignment.

## End of shift

1. Confirm that completed deliveries have the correct status.
2. Report unresolved deliveries to dispatch.
3. Set availability to Offline.
4. Stop GPS Tracking.
5. Logout if the phone is shared or company policy requires it.

# 15. Troubleshooting

| Problem or message | What to do |
|---|---|
| Invalid email or password | Re-enter both values carefully. Use Forgot password? if needed. |
| Email not confirmed | Open the confirmation email or use Resend confirmation email. Check Spam/Junk. |
| Too many login attempts | Wait a few minutes before trying again. |
| Registration field is highlighted | Correct that field using the example/format requested by the app. |
| Registration failed because the email is already registered | Return to Login, confirm the email, or reset the password. |
| Dashboard does not show a new delivery | Check internet access, tap refresh, pull down to refresh, and inspect Notifications. |
| No active deliveries | Confirm availability, refresh, and contact dispatch if work was expected. |
| Could not update status | Check internet access, refresh the delivery, and try once more. Do not repeatedly select different statuses. |
| Location services are disabled | Open Settings > Device GPS and enable Location. |
| Location permission denied | Open Settings > App Location Permission and allow location access. |
| Could not get GPS location | Move outdoors or near a window, keep Device GPS on, and retry. |
| Location saved locally; sync failed | Check the internet connection and keep the app open while it reconnects. |
| Map has fewer markers than deliveries | Some delivery addresses may not contain usable coordinates. Read the written addresses and notify dispatch if required. |
| Notifications do not appear on the phone | Enable Android notifications for SC Courier, check internet access, and inspect the in-app notification inbox. |
| Profile cannot be saved | Complete highlighted required fields and retry with a stable internet connection. |
| Profile photo was skipped | The text details were saved, but photo storage may be unavailable. Retry later or contact support. |
| App startup failed | Record the displayed error, check the internet connection, restart the app, and contact technical support if it continues. |

When reporting a problem, provide the rider email, delivery reference, approximate time, and a screenshot of the message. Never send your password.

# 16. Safety, privacy, and account security

- Do not operate the app while riding or driving. Stop in a safe location first.
- Verify the recipient before handing over a parcel.
- Treat recipient names, phone numbers, addresses, and parcel information as confidential.
- Use GPS tracking only for authorised work purposes.
- Never share your password, confirmation link, or password-reset link.
- Do not copy customer information into personal messaging or social-media apps unless company procedure specifically requires it.
- Report a lost phone or suspected account access immediately.
- Keep Android, the SC Courier app, and screen-lock protection up to date.
